# Changelog

All notable changes to Package Tycoon. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/):
`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`: major = big overhauls or save-breaking changes, minor = new features, patch = bug fixes.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-17
First official release. 🎉

### Gameplay
- Drag-and-drop package sorting on a 3D conveyor belt with bins for NYC, LA, MIAMI, CHI and SEA
- Trucks ship full bins for cash; reputation multiplies payouts
- Daily shifts with rent, wages and truck upkeep; contracts, rush hours and belt jams
- Package types: standard, ★ priority, ⚠ fragile and ❄ perishable

### Tycoon systems
- Staff: sorters, robo-sorters, sorting drones, dispatcher, technician and manager
- Manager autopilot and auto-invest, so the warehouse can run itself
- Nine facility upgrades
- Research Lab: 12 techs across Automation, Logistics and Business
- Franchise prestige: trophies give permanent income bonuses; Legacy Perks

### Online
- 1v1 Versus over the internet with room codes (PeerJS, encrypted WebRTC)
- Seeded fair days, sabotage abilities (jam, junk flood, blackout, shield), emotes, rematch
- Version check warns when players run different versions

### Look & sound
- Cozy cartoon UI theme and toon-shaded pastel 3D world with outlines
- Generative lo-fi soundtrack (seamless loop) with calm, full and muffled modes
- Conveyor hum, automation sounds, trucks, alarms and more, with stereo panning
- Settings: sound, music and effects volume, shadows, hints, pause-while-managing
